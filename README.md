# Pidgin-Vector-Privacy-Policy
Pidgin Vector App Privacy Policy
Introduction
Andrew Gilfillan, d/b/a Pidgin Vector ("us", "we", "our" or the "Company"), values your privacy and the importance of safeguarding your data. This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") describes how we collect, store, access, and otherwise process information relating to individuals who use the Pidgin Vector mobile application (the "App"). In this Policy, personal data ("Personal Data") refers to any information that on its own, or in combination with other available information, can identify an individual.
Pidgin Vector is a mapping app for viewing, creating, editing, and exporting shapefiles and other vector map data. It is designed so that the map files you open and create are processed on your device, not uploaded to us.

Personal Data We Collect
We collect as little Personal Data as possible. Pidgin Vector does not require you to create an account, and we do not sell your Personal Data.
Map Files and Project Data
Shapefiles and other map files you import, create, edit, or export (including their geometry, attributes, and projection settings) are stored and processed on your device. We do not upload, view, or store your map files on our servers. When you export or share a file, it goes only to the destination you choose (for example, the Files app, email, or cloud storage), and that destination's own privacy policy applies.
Location Data
If you grant permission, the App may use your device's location to show your position on the map or to help you create features at your current location. Location is processed on your device and is not sent to us. You can turn location access off at any time in your device settings; the rest of the App will keep working.
Subscription and Payment Information
Subscriptions and free trials are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple collects and processes your payment details under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or store your credit card, debit card, or billing address. We may receive limited transaction information from Apple, such as whether your subscription or trial is active, which we use only to unlock paid features.
Information You Send Us
If you contact us by email, we collect your email address, your name if you provide it, and the contents of your message, including any files or screenshots you choose to attach. We use this only to respond to you.
Device and Usage Data
We may receive anonymous, aggregated diagnostic information, such as crash reports and general usage statistics, through Apple if you have opted in to share analytics with app developers in your device settings. This information does not identify you personally.
If you provide us with Personal Data about other individuals, you represent that you have the authority to do so. If you believe your Personal Data has been provided to us improperly, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section.
Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing
We use the limited Personal Data described above only to:
• Provide the App and unlock the features of your trial or subscription
• Respond to your questions, feedback, and support requests
• Fix bugs and improve the App's stability and performance
• Comply with legal obligations and protect against fraud
Where we process Personal Data to provide the App or your subscription, we do so because it is necessary to perform our contract with you. Where we use location data, we rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in your device settings. Other processing, such as fixing bugs and preventing fraud, is based on our legitimate interest in running and protecting the App. If we change the type of data we collect, we will ask for your consent again where required.
International Data Transfer and Storage
Because your map files and location data stay on your device, they are not transferred to us. The limited data we do handle, such as support emails and information received from Apple, may be processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your country. Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell, rent, or trade your Personal Data, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share Personal Data only as described below.
Service Providers. We rely on Apple to distribute the App and process subscriptions, and on an email provider to receive and answer support messages. These providers process data on our behalf or under their own privacy policies.
Legal Requirement. We may disclose Personal Data to comply with a legal obligation, a valid request from a public authority, or court proceedings; to prevent loss of life or injury; or to protect our rights or property. Where possible and practical, we will tell you in advance.
Cookies and Tracking
The App does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, or third-party tracking tools, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
Retention and Deletion
Map files and settings stored in the App remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. We keep support emails only as long as needed to resolve your request and as required by law. When we no longer need Personal Data, we delete or anonymize it.
Merger or Acquisition
If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your Personal Data may be transferred. We will give notice before it is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.
How We Keep Your Data Safe
We use appropriate organizational and technical safeguards to protect Personal Data from accidental loss and from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure. Files stored in the App are protected by your device's built-in security, such as your passcode and encryption. Any communication with us involving Personal Data uses an encrypted connection where available. If a Personal Data breach occurs, we will notify you and any applicable regulator when legally required.
Children's Privacy
The App is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children under 16. If you believe a child has sent us Personal Data, contact us and we will delete it.
Your Rights for Your Personal Data
Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights:
• Right to Access (PIPEDA, GDPR Art. 15, CCPA/CPRA, CPA, VCDPA, CTDPA, UCPA, TDPSA, LGPD, POPIA). You may ask whether we process your Personal Data and request a copy.
• Right to Rectification (PIPEDA, GDPR Art. 16, CPRA, CPA, VCDPA, CTDPA, TDPSA, LGPD, POPIA). You may have inaccurate or incomplete Personal Data corrected.
• Right to Erasure (GDPR Art. 17, CCPA/CPRA, CPA, VCDPA, CTDPA, UCPA, TDPSA, LGPD, POPIA). You may ask us to delete your Personal Data, unless we must keep it to meet a legal obligation or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
• Right to Restriction of Processing (GDPR Art. 18, LGPD). You may ask us to limit processing to storage only in certain circumstances.
• Right to Portability (PIPEDA, GDPR Art. 20, LGPD). You may obtain Personal Data you provided to us in a structured, electronic format. Your map files are already stored on your device and can be exported at any time.
• Right to Opt Out (CPRA, CPA, VCDPA, CTDPA, UCPA, TDPSA). You may opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of Personal Data, and certain profiling. We do not engage in any of these activities.
• Right to Object (GDPR Art. 21, LGPD, POPIA). You may object to processing based on our legitimate interests. We will stop unless we have compelling legitimate grounds or need the data for a legal claim.
• Nondiscrimination (CCPA/CPRA, CPA, VCDPA, CTDPA, UCPA, TDPSA). We will not deny you service or change your experience for exercising your rights.
• Right to Appeal (CPA, VCDPA, CTDPA, TDPSA and other U.S. state laws). If we decline your request, you may appeal by emailing us. If you disagree with our decision on appeal, you may contact your state attorney general.
• Right to File a Complaint (GDPR Art. 77, LGPD, POPIA). You may complain to your local data protection authority. EEA residents can find theirs here: https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en
• Withdrawing Consent. Where we rely on your consent, such as for location access, you may withdraw it at any time, free of charge, in your device settings or by contacting us.

For your security, we may ask you to verify your identity before acting on your request. To cancel a subscription, use your Apple ID subscription settings; deleting the App does not cancel it.
