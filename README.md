# Pidgin Vector Privacy Policy
Effective date: September 30, 2026
Pidgin Vector is an offline field GIS tool for viewing, creating, and exporting map data on iPhone. This policy describes what information the app handles. The short version: the app does not collect, transmit, or share your personal information.
Data we collect
None. Pidgin Vector has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash-reporting services. The app contains no third-party software development kits. It does not sell, rent, or share any information, because no information about you ever reaches us.
Location
The app can use your location only to center the map or follow your position while you work ("Locate" button). Location data is processed on your device, shown on your screen, and never transmitted to us or to anyone else. It is not stored in your project files or exports. You can deny location permission and the app works fine without it; you can change this any time in iOS Settings.
Data stored on your device
Everything you create stays on your device:
Projects and map features (drawn shapes, imported files, generated lines) are saved in the app's own storage space on your device.
Settings (basemap choice, layer toggles) are stored on your device.
Reference layers (state, county, and country outlines) are bundled inside the app itself, so they work with no internet connection.
Undo history exists only in the app's memory while it runs.
We have no access to any of this. Deleting the app deletes all of it.
Network activity
The app makes exactly one kind of internet request: when you use the county boundary tools, it asks the U.S. Census Bureau's public TIGERweb service (tigerweb.geo.census.gov) for county outline data. That request contains only a county identification number. It contains no information about you or your device. Responses are cached on your device to reduce repeat downloads.
Map imagery is provided by Apple Maps under Apple's own privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The app sends Apple the map area you are viewing, as any maps app does. No other network connections are made. Exports and imports are prepared entirely on your device.
Purchases
Subscriptions are sold and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using Apple's StoreKit system. The app never receives your name, payment details, or billing information — it only asks Apple whether an active subscription exists for this device's App Store account. The 3-day free trial, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's terms. We run no subscription server and keep no purchase records.
Children
Pidgin Vector is a professional mapping tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and since it collects no data, it collects no data from children.
Your rights
Because the app collects no personal information, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export, or delete on your behalf — including under the California Consumer Privacy Act or the EU General Data Protection Regulation. Your data is your project files, on your device, under your control.
Changes to this policy
If a future version of the app changes any of the above, we will update this page and the App Store privacy labels before that version ships.
